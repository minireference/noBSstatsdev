Meet the author behind the madness

Good explanations don't happen by accident.

Ivan Savov, the author behind the No Bullshit Guide textbooks. His writing brings together years of tutoring experience and a conviction that technical ideas can be explained clearly, without watering them down.

He holds a B.Eng. in electrical engineering, an M.Sc. in physics, and a Ph.D. in computer science from McGill University. Ivan has also published several other successful books. His No Bullshit Guide to Linear algebra and math and physics have become extremely useful and become a big hit.
